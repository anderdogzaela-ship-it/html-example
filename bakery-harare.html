Our Story

Rooted in Harare,
Baked with Purpose

What started as a small family kitchen in Avondale has grown into one of Harare's most beloved bakeries. Every recipe carries sixteen years of refinement, local ingredients, and community pride.

Locally Sourced Ingredients
We partner with Zimbabwean farms for flour, eggs, dairy, and seasonal fruits.
Baked Fresh Daily
Our ovens start at 4am so your bread and pastries arrive warm every morning.
