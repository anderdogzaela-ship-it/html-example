Everything in one app

Two complete experiences - dog owners and walkers - built into a single production-grade platform.

Smart Booking System
Real-time availability, multi-dog support, duration picker and instant confirmation with automated reminders.
Live Walk Tracking
GPS route tracking, live photo updates, and walk duration visible to the owner in real time.
Secure Payments
Stripe Connect holds funds at booking and releases to the walker only after the walk completes.
Ratings and Reviews
Two-way review system after each walk builds trust and surfaces the best walkers organically.
Push Notifications
Booking requests, walk start, live updates and payment confirmation - all delivered instantly.
Walker Earnings Dashboard
Weekly earnings summary, completed walks, rating history and payout schedule for every walker.

Built on a production stack

Wait - Flutter (iOS + Android)
Firebase Auth + Firestore
Stripe Connect
Google Maps SDK
Firebase Push Notifications
Cloud Functions (Node.js)
