The problem today

Why manual dispatch breaks down as orders grow

Phone calls and a notebook work for a handful of daily orders. Past that, plants lose visibility and customers are left guessing.

Phone and manual dispatch
  • No visibility into which tanker is actually free right now
  • Orders tracked by hand, easy to double-book or lose
  • Customer has no idea when the tanker will actually arrive
  • Plant has no reliable record of daily dispatch volume
AquaFlow, one connected system
  • Every tanker status visible live, no double-booking
  • Orders move through a clear status sequence, end to end
  • Customer sees a live ETA from confirmation to delivery
  • Plant gets a full dispatch history automatically
Three roles, one platform

Customer, plant and driver on the same order in real time

Each role gets an interface built for what they actually do all day, all reading and writing the same order record.

Customer app

Order water in a few taps, choose a plant or let the system pick the nearest one.

  • Live tanker tracking
  • Delivery history
  • Repeat a previous order

Water plant admin

Manage tanker availability, incoming orders and daily dispatch from one screen.

  • Inventory & tanker status
  • Live order queue
  • Dispatch reports

Tanker operator app

Drivers receive orders, confirm pickup and get navigation straight to the drop-off point.

  • One-tap accept
  • Turn-by-turn navigation
  • Delivery confirmation

Operator app

Drivers stay in control of their day

Operators toggle availability, see today's assigned deliveries and confirm every drop-off directly in the app.

  • 1
    One queue, clear priority

    Orders arrive sorted by distance and urgency, no manual radio calls between plant and driver.

  • 2
    Live navigation to every stop

    Turn-by-turn directions straight from the app, no separate maps app to juggle mid-delivery.

  • 3
    Delivery confirmation built in

    A photo or signature at drop-off closes the order automatically for the plant and the customer.
