Our Story

Two worlds.
One cup.

Sora started from a simple question: why do coffee and matcha still feel like separate worlds? We believe they belong together — not as a gimmick, but as a genuine conversation between two ancient drink cultures.

We source ceremonial-grade matcha directly from Uji, Japan, and work with single-origin coffee farms whose beans we rotate seasonally. Every drink at Sora is a chance to slow down.

Organic & traceable. Every ingredient sourced with full transparency.
Community first. A space for regulars, remote workers, and curious newcomers.
Zero-waste mindset. Compostable packaging and in-house composting program.
